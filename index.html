How it works

Three steps, no site visit

Step 1

Send a sketch and a few photos

Paper, a tablet note or a phone photo is fine. Mark the counter length, depth, heights, the passage width, and where the window, door and fan are.

Step 2

We model it and check the fit

We lay out cabinets and drawers, place your appliances using their real sizes, and flag anything that won't fit before a single board is cut.

Step 3

You get a 3D link and a print set

Share the 3D model with family on any phone. Hand the printed drawings to your carpenter: plan, elevations, drawer heights, cabinet schedule and services.

Why check first

What we caught on this one kitchen

Dishwasher

The opening under the slab was 22″. A built-in dishwasher needs about 32″. Caught on paper, not after delivery.

Sloping slab

The platform dropped from 27″ to 26″ underneath. Cabinets were built level with a scribed strip instead of being cut to fit on site.

Ceiling fan

The fan blades sat level with the upper cabinets. Uppers were set to stop 3″ below the blades.

Gas hose

The cylinder-to-stove hose got its own 2″ channel behind the drawers, kept under the 1.5 m limit.

Questions

Before you send a sketch

Do you visit the site?

No. We work from your sketch, photos and measurements. The drawings list what your carpenter should re-measure before cutting.

Inches or millimetres?

Inches by default, since most carpenters here work in inches. Board and banding thicknesses stay in mm, the way they are sold.

Do you build the kitchen?

No. You keep your own carpenter and buy your own materials. We give you drawings he can follow.

How long does it take?

Usually 2–3 working days for the first version, then a day for each round of changes.
